My Trip to Fire Island

My recent getaway to Fire Island was absolutely unforgettable. From the pristine, vehicle-free sandy beaches to the historic lighthouse, every moment felt like a peaceful escape from the hustle and bustle of daily life. The highlight of the trip was watching the breathtaking sunset over the ocean and exploring the unique sunken forest ecosystem.
